steam_api64.dll
===============

steam_api64.dll is Valve Corporation's Steam API library, the 64-bit
redistributable of the Steamworks SDK (version 1.65,
sdk/redistributable_bin/win64/steam_api64.dll). It is not part of
ffx-rust and not under its licence (MIT OR Apache-2.0): it is
Copyright (c) Valve Corporation, all rights reserved, and is
redistributed with ffx-rust under the terms of the Steamworks SDK Access
Agreement (https://partner.steamgames.com/documentation/sdk_access_agreement),
which allows the SDK's redistributable files to be shipped with an
application that uses them.

ffx-rust.exe loads it at run time to talk to the Steam client
(achievements). Without it, or without Steam running, ffx-rust.exe runs
with Steam off. SteamAPI_RestartAppIfNecessary and ResetAllStats are never
called through it.
