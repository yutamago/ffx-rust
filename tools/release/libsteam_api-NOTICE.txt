libsteam_api.so / libsteam_api.dylib
====================================

libsteam_api.so (Linux) and libsteam_api.dylib (macOS) are Valve
Corporation's Steam API library, the redistributables of the Steamworks
SDK (version 1.65, sdk/redistributable_bin/linux64, linuxarm64 and osx).
They are not part of ffx-rust and not under its licence (MIT OR
Apache-2.0): they are Copyright (c) Valve Corporation, all rights
reserved, and are redistributed with ffx-rust under the terms of the
Steamworks SDK Access Agreement
(https://partner.steamgames.com/documentation/sdk_access_agreement),
which allows the SDK's redistributable files to be shipped with an
application that uses them.

ffx-rust loads it at run time to talk to the Steam client
(achievements). Without it, or without Steam running, ffx-rust runs with
Steam off. SteamAPI_RestartAppIfNecessary and ResetAllStats are never
called through it.
