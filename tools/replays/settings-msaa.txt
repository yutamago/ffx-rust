# Graphics settings (M4 coverage 2, docs/harness.md "Settings replays"):
# field-walk's deck (save ffx_001, cdsp0000) with MSAA=4
# (tools/sandbox/msaa4.ini merged over the sandbox's GameSetting.ini,
# Quality=VQ_CUSTOM so the file's value counts). Reaches
# PostProcessManager_setMSAA with 4 samples, the multisampled targets, their
# resolves and the scene's Opaque pass instead of OpaqueNoMSAA
# (g_Settings +0x38). Same walk, shots and traces as settings-hdao.txt.
