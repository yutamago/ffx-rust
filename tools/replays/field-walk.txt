# Field: load save slot 1 (ffx_001, salvage ship deck, cdsp0000) without the title menu, trace,
# walk about and talk. Ranges: the global random generator (0x00c42200),
# the 68 generators at 0x01135ee0, the party records (0x0113205c, 18 x
# 0x94), the pad word (0x00ccb170). Screenshots before and after the
# walk (field-walk-<n>.bmp; models must be drawn).
load direct 1 cdsp0000
lockstep on
# The map fades in over about a second after the direct load. The shot is
# pinned to the event's clock, not to a frame count: the asset loader takes
# a varying number of frames, and the map's animations (the searchlight
# sweep, the deck's sway) start with the event. 0x01325d94 is +0x1f4 of
# ctrl work 0 (g_AtelCtrlWorks 0x01325ba0, 0x238 a work): the frames its
# event has run (Atel_ExecCtrlWorks 0x008688f0 counts it; AtelRequireCtrlWork
# 0x0086ee10 zeroes it). 120 frames after the load it read 105-111 on an idle
# machine and 40-58 under load (scores 17-20 against the recording); at the
# same count, runs give the same picture. 101 matches the recording (0.1;
# 98: 0.6, 110: 2.2). The wait is for the count to enter 64..127 (mask
# 0xffffffc0) and then 37 frames, so a count that skipped a value cannot
# be missed; the 1500-frame limit fails the replay when the event never
# runs.
waitfor 0x01325d94:4 40000000 c0ffffff 1500
wait 37
read 0x01325d94 4
shot $OUT\field-walk-1.bmp
trace 0x00c42200:4 0x01135ee0:0x110 0x0113205c:0xa68 0x00ccb170:8
press up 40 10
press left 20 10
press down 40 10
press right 30 10
press circle 4 30
press circle 4 30
# in lockstep the presses and waits take a fixed 358 frames: this shot is at
# event frame 459 (read back into the replay's output, 0x1cb)
wait 120
read 0x01325d94 4
shot $OUT\field-walk-2.bmp
