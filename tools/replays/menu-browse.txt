# Menu: load save slot 1 (ffx_001, salvage ship deck) without the title menu, open the main
# menu and walk through its screens (items, abilities, status, config),
# back out and close it. Ranges: the pad word (0x00ccb170) and the party
# records (0x0113205c, 18 x 0x94); the window tables hold heap pointers,
# which differ between any two runs. Screenshots at fixed frames go to
# $OUT\menu-browse-<n>.bmp (the trace's folder), for tools/shot-compare.py.
load direct 1 cdsp0000
lockstep on
# what the frames before lockstep leave behind: docs/harness.md,
# "Pinned state"
pin
# FFX_AUDIO_WAV (run-all.sh --audio): the capture is compared from the
# `audiomark start` to the `audiomark end` (docs/harness.md, "Audio capture")
audiomark start
# the field takes input only after its fade-in; 30 frames was too few
# under parallel load (the menu stayed shut)
wait 120
trace 0x00ccb170:8 0x0113205c:0xa68
press triangle 4 40
shot $OUT\menu-browse-1.bmp
press down 4 15
press down 4 15
press circle 4 40
shot $OUT\menu-browse-2.bmp
press down 4 15
press down 4 15
press right 4 15
press cross 4 30
press down 4 15
press circle 4 40
shot $OUT\menu-browse-3.bmp
press cross 4 30
press down 4 15
press down 4 15
press circle 4 40
shot $OUT\menu-browse-4.bmp
press cross 4 30
press cross 4 30
press triangle 4 30
wait 60
shot $OUT\menu-browse-5.bmp
audiomark end
