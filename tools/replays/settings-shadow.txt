# Graphics settings (M4 coverage 2, docs/harness.md "Settings replays"):
# Shadow=3 (tools/sandbox/shadow.ini merged over the sandbox's
# GameSetting.ini, Quality=VQ_CUSTOM so the file's value counts), on three
# maps with three shadow solutions (docs/formats/scene-maps.md, Q7): the
# deck (save ffx_001, cdsp0000, solution 2: one cascade), Besaid beach
# (save ffx_003, bsil0000, solution 4: two cascades) and the Thunder Plains
# agency (save ffx_005, kami0100, solution 0: the per-character atlas).
# With Shadow=0 ShadowManager +0x4060 is 0 and the cascade pass 0x006c9e50
# and 0x006caee0 return at once; here they draw. Shots at ctrl work 0's
# count 101 on each map (and the deck's after field-walk's short walk).
# Traced: the settings words (g_Settings +0x30..+0x4b), the map words, the
# generators and the party records.
settings shadow
load direct 1 cdsp0000 lockstep
lockstep on
pin
waitfor 0x01325d94:4 40000000 c0ffffff 1500
wait 37
readp 0x022fb504 0x30 0x1c
trace *0x022fb504+0x30:0x1c 0x0112ca90:4 0x00c42200:4 0x01135ee0:0x110 0x0113205c:0xa68
read 0x01325d94 4
shot $OUT\settings-shadow-1.bmp
press up 40 10
press left 20 10
press down 20 10
wait 120
read 0x01325d94 4
shot $OUT\settings-shadow-2.bmp
# Besaid beach, as draw-field.txt's third capture
loadsave 3
waitfor 0x0112ca90:2 4600 ffff 1500
waitfor 0x01325d94:4 00000000 c0ffffff 1500
waitfor 0x01325d94:4 40000000 c0ffffff 1500
wait 37

shot $OUT\settings-shadow-3.bmp
# the agency
