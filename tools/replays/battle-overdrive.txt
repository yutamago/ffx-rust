# Battle overdrive and summon: Tidus's Spiral Cut with the timing bar hit,
# Yuna summons Valefor (entry, party swap), Valefor's overdrive Energy
# Blast, Valefor dismissed (the party back), Wakka's menu last.
#
# Save slot 5 (ffx_005, story 1470, the Moonflow `kami0100`: Valefor, Ifrit
# and Ixion joined), battle field 33 (`genk16`, the Djose highroad, on
# land) group 0 formation 3 (`genk16_03`: Ochu, Garm). The state is forced
# by writes, not taken from the save:
# - the battle party: g_SaveInParty (0x011307e8, save +0x3d58, the first 3
#   of 20 ply_save indices; MsGetSaveInParty 0x00785270) written to Yuna,
#   Tidus, Wakka (1, 0, 4; the rest of the list kept, Yuna's old slot 7
#   given to Rikku 6) before the battle starts;
# - the overdrive gauges: actor (*0x011334d4 g_BtlChrTop + index x 0xf90,
#   MsGetChr 0x00794020; party actors are indexed by character, the
#   monsters from 0x14) +0x5bc (MsGetChrLimitGauge 0x00795550; +0x5bd the
#   max, MsGetChrLimitGaugeMax 0x00795590, +0x5bb the type) set to the max:
#   Tidus (0) 100, Valefor (8, +0x823c) 20;
# - the Ochu (actor 0x14) gets 99999 HP (+0x5d0, max +0x594, as
#   docs/harness.md "Observed"), so Energy Blast does not end the battle
#   and Valefor can be dismissed. With its own 7200 the Ochu dies to it;
#   and a longer script (dismiss, Wakka, Tidus, summon again) lets the
#   Ochu KO Yuna first, as the random draws fall.
# Whose menu is open: g_BtlWindowOpen (0x023cc08c, the window character's
# bit; TOSetBtlActiveChr 0x0089bb10): Tidus 1, Yuna 2, Wakka 0x10, Valefor
# 0x100. The menus remember their cursor (TOBtlMemoryCursorPos).
# The timing bar (TOBtlCtrlTidusLimitWindow 0x00892320, state i16
# 0x0133d734, result 0x0133d6f3): in state 4 the marker (i16 0x0133d742)
# moves by 11 (0x0133d744) from 118 (0x0133d736) over 275 (0x0133d73a);
# a circle press while it is in 243..267 (0x0133d73c..0x0133d73e, at
# 1280x720) hits. The script waits for the marker to read 250 (0xfa) and
# presses: the next frame checks 250 (or 261 one frame later), both in.
# Effects are pinned to their magic files (g_MagicFile_Handle 0x00c64cac,
# low half 0 once loaded, -1 before; the magic ID 0x00c64ca0): Valefor's
# entry 0x253, Energy Blast 0x25c. Spiral Cut (0x186) is loaded while the
# bar runs and plays when the bar's state is back at 0.
#
# Ranges, from Tidus's first menu on (whether it opens on the frame the
# phase byte turns 1 or the next varies between runs of the original): the
# magic ID, the handle's low half (the high half is the module base, which
# moves between runs), the timing bar's result (not its state: states 3
# and 5 last one frame, and one of five original runs skipped 3), the
# window bits, g_BtlSummoner (0x0112c9e8), Tidus's and Valefor's gauges
# (+0x5bc:2), the HP (+0x5d0) of Tidus, Yuna, Wakka, Valefor, the Ochu
# (0x14) and the Garm (0x15), the battle phase byte (0x0112a8e0).
# Screenshots (battle-overdrive-<n>.bmp): 1 the timing bar, 2 Spiral Cut,
# 3 and 4 Valefor's entry, 5 Valefor's menu, 6 and 7 Energy Blast, 8 the
# dismissal, 9 the party back (Wakka's menu).
load direct 5 kami0100
lockstep on
wait 10
write 0x011307e8 01000405ff020306
battle 33 0 3
waitfor 0x0112a8e0:1 01 ff 4000
writep 0x011334d4 0x5bc 64
writep 0x011334d4 0x823c 14
writep 0x011334d4 0x13cd4 9f860100
writep 0x011334d4 0x13d10 9f860100
# Tidus: Swordplay (left), Spiral Cut, the Ochu
waitfor 0x023cc08c:4 01000000 ffffffff 600
trace 0x00c64ca0:4 0x00c64cac:2 0x0133d6f3:1 0x023cc08c:4 0x0112c9e8:1 *0x011334d4+0x5bc:2 *0x011334d4+0x823c:2 *0x011334d4+0x5d0:4 *0x011334d4+0x1560:4 *0x011334d4+0x4410:4 *0x011334d4+0x8250:4 *0x011334d4+0x13d10:4 *0x011334d4+0x14ca0:4 0x0112a8e0:1
wait 30
press left 4 30
press circle 4 30
press circle 4 30
press circle 4 10
waitfor 0x0133d742:2 fa00 ffff 600
shot $OUT\battle-overdrive-1.bmp
press circle 4 30
waitfor 0x0133d734:2 0000 ffff 600
wait 60
shot $OUT\battle-overdrive-2.bmp
# Yuna: Summon, Valefor
waitfor 0x023cc08c:4 02000000 ffffffff 3000
wait 30
press down 4 30
press circle 4 30
press circle 4 0
waitfor 0x00c64ca0:4 53020000 ffffffff 600
waitfor 0x00c64cac:2 0000 ffff 1200
wait 150
shot $OUT\battle-overdrive-3.bmp
wait 100
shot $OUT\battle-overdrive-4.bmp
# Valefor: Overdrive (left), Energy Blast, all enemies
waitfor 0x023cc08c:4 00010000 ffffffff 3000
wait 30
shot $OUT\battle-overdrive-5.bmp
press left 4 30
press circle 4 30
press circle 4 0
waitfor 0x00c64ca0:4 5c020000 ffffffff 600
waitfor 0x00c64cac:2 0000 ffff 1200
wait 150
shot $OUT\battle-overdrive-6.bmp
wait 70
shot $OUT\battle-overdrive-7.bmp
# Valefor again: Dismiss (right, down twice), on itself
waitfor 0x023cc08c:4 00010000 ffffffff 3000
wait 30
press right 4 30
press down 4 30
press down 4 30
press circle 4 30
press circle 4 0
wait 120
shot $OUT\battle-overdrive-8.bmp
# the party back: the next party member's menu
waitfor 0x023cc08c:4 10000000 ffffffff 3000
wait 30
shot $OUT\battle-overdrive-9.bmp
