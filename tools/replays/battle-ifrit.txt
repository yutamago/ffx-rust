# Another battle and summon (M4 coverage 2): the Thunder Plains (battle
# field 34, kami00, battle map 0x412 with its rain and lightning; formation
# kami00_00: Larva and Gold Element) from save ffx_005, as
# battle-overdrive.txt starts (party Yuna, Tidus, Wakka; the monsters' HP
# and max HP set to 99999 so the battle runs on). Tidus attacks, then Yuna
# summons Ifrit (Summon, the second aeon): its entry (Ifrit is actor 9;
# magic ID 0x254; Ifrit's effect state, actor +0xdfb, goes from 1 to 5 within a
# few frames, never 4 at a frame boundary, so the shots count from 5).
# Lockstep, fast mode, pinned. Traced: the magic ID and file handle's low
# half, the window bits, the summoner, the party's and the monsters' HP,
# the battle phase. Shots (battle-ifrit-<n>.bmp): 1 Tidus's menu, 2 Yuna's
# summon list, 3 and 4 Ifrit's entry.
load direct 5 kami0100
lockstep on
pin
wait 10
write 0x011307e8 01000405ff020306
battle 34 0 0
waitfor 0x0112a8e0:1 01 ff 4000
# FFX_AUDIO_WAV (run-all.sh --audio): the capture is compared from the
# `audiomark start` to the `audiomark end` (docs/harness.md, "Audio
# capture"). It starts in the battle: the Thunder Plains' field music began
# before lockstep, in real time, and its phase at lockstep differed between
# original runs (the first 60 blocks, until it faded out for the battle).
audiomark start
writep 0x011334d4 0x13cd4 9f860100
writep 0x011334d4 0x13d10 9f860100
writep 0x011334d4 0x14c64 9f860100
writep 0x011334d4 0x14ca0 9f860100
waitfor 0x023cc08c:4 01000000 ffffffff 600
trace 0x00c64ca0:4 0x00c64cac:2 0x023cc08c:4 0x0112c9e8:1 *0x011334d4+0x5d0:4 *0x011334d4+0x1560:4 *0x011334d4+0x4410:4 *0x011334d4+0x13d10:4 *0x011334d4+0x14ca0:4 0x0112a8e0:1
wait 30
shot $OUT\battle-ifrit-1.bmp
press circle 4 30
press circle 4 30
waitfor 0x023cc08c:4 02000000 ffffffff 3000
wait 30
press down 4 30
press circle 4 30
press down 4 30
shot $OUT\battle-ifrit-2.bmp
press circle 4 0
waitfor 0x00c64ca0:4 54020000 ffffffff 600
waitfor *0x011334d4+0x9a0b:1 05 ff 1500
wait 150
shot $OUT\battle-ifrit-3.bmp
wait 100
shot $OUT\battle-ifrit-4.bmp
